# Converge identity usage

## Brand idea

**Settlement Fold** represents controlled autonomy:

1. the outer band is the governed execution boundary;
2. the open threshold is work that has not yet settled;
3. the gold fold is the closing proof;
4. the compact silhouette is a stamp, not a flowchart.

The primary brand line is **Compile intent into shipped software.**

The short proof line is **Done is proven.**

## Primary marks

- Use the dark full-color mark on white, Proof Ivory, and other light surfaces.
- Use the light full-color mark on Factory Black, Carbon, and other dark surfaces.
- Prefer the horizontal lockup when the Converge name is not already visible nearby.
- Prefer the icon alone for favicons, app icons, avatars, compact navigation, and state stamps.

## Clear space

Keep clear space of at least one fold width around every edge of the icon. For the horizontal
lockup, apply the same unit above, below, and on both ends.

Do not place text, borders, controls, or other marks inside this exclusion zone.

## Minimum size

- Primary icon: 24 px digital minimum.
- Prepared favicon: 16 px only in browser favicon contexts.
- Horizontal lockup: 180 px wide digital minimum.
- Print icon: 8 mm minimum.

At small sizes, use the supplied exports. Do not shrink the 1024 px master ad hoc and then
recompress it.

## Color

- Forge Gold is the identity accent and the fold color.
- Settlement Mint is reserved for verified or settled states.
- Verdict Coral is reserved for RED, refused, failed, or human-attention states.
- Never use Mint or Coral as a replacement brand accent in the primary logo.
- For one-color production, render the entire mark in one approved color.

## Typography

The wordmark is custom vector geometry. Never retype or reconstruct it with a font.

For product and communication typography:

- Display/UI: `Avenir Next`, `Inter`, `ui-sans-serif`, `system-ui`, sans-serif.
- Technical/data: `Menlo`, `IBM Plex Mono`, `ui-monospace`, monospace.

Font files are not bundled. Use appropriately licensed local or webfont installations.

## Do

- Use the supplied SVG when a vector asset is supported.
- Preserve the exact proportion between the body and gold fold.
- Keep the icon upright.
- Use full-color artwork when production permits.
- Use the supplied social, PWA, favicon, and responsive-web exports.
- Treat `MANIFEST.sha256` as the package integrity ledger.

## Avoid

- Do not stretch, rotate, skew, crop, outline, bevel, or add a drop shadow.
- Do not redraw the fold, close the opening, or turn the symbol into a cube.
- Do not add convergence rails, arrows, checkmarks, shields, or extra diamonds.
- Do not place the dark mark on a dark surface or the light mark on a light surface.
- Do not recolor individual pieces outside the canonical palette.
- Do not use the Round 01 concept sheet as production artwork.

## Product-state applications

The symbol may use a semantic fold color in state-specific product UI:

- Gold: `BOUND` — proving, not yet settled.
- Mint: `SETTLED` — verified and shipped.
- Coral: `RED` — refused, failed, or correction required.

These are UI states. The master-brand logo remains Forge Gold.
