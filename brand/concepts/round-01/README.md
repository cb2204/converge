# Converge Logo Exploration — Round 01

Status: **concept exploration only — no logo is approved**

These sheets isolate the logo decision before production brand-kit work begins. They are
AI-assisted raster explorations, not final master artwork. The selected direction must be
redrawn as controlled SVG geometry and tested again before it becomes the Converge mark.

## Strategic brief

- Product idea: compile intent into shipped software.
- Brand promise: controlled autonomy; `done` is proven rather than claimed.
- Desired character: exacting, calm, formidable, trustworthy.
- Existing visual equity: many lineage streams narrowing into one proven outcome.
- Primary accent: Forge Gold `#F3B64C`.

## Candidates

### A — Proof Gate V2

A geometric `C`, three converging input rails, and a terminal proof diamond.

- Strength: tells the Converge product story most directly.
- Risk: the rail geometry can make the mark read as an `E` or euro-like glyph.

### A3 — Proof Gate, contained

The same idea with the convergence geometry moved inside the `C`.

- Strength: keeps the outer boundary cleaner and more compact.
- Risk: the internal channels still need a manual redraw to protect the `C` reading at small sizes.

### B — Settlement Fold

A continuous folded ribbon intended to express a bounded loop reaching closure.

- Strength: compact and simple in one color.
- Risk: reads close to a familiar `G` / hexagonal technology mark and is less specific to Converge.

### C — Consensus Barrier

Two opposing architectural gates around a protected kernel.

- Strength: expresses the Pass 4 barrier and referee role.
- Risk: reads as a bowtie or generic gateway and loses the master-brand initial.

### D — C/V Threshold

A compact `C` monogram with a `V`-shaped verdict/descent cut.

- Strength: bold and highly compact.
- Risk: can read as download, power, or shield symbolism.

## Current recommendation

Advance **A / A3** and manually simplify it into one master:

1. keep the `C` as the dominant silhouette;
2. reduce the three channels to two or turn them into one negative-space convergence;
3. keep the proof diamond at the open gate;
4. test one-color first at 16, 24, and 32 px;
5. reject any redraw that reads as `E`, euro, fish, eye, or play button.

## Approval gate

Do not build the full brand kit until one direction is explicitly selected. Approval of a
concept authorizes a vector redraw round; it does not approve the raster sheet as production
artwork.
